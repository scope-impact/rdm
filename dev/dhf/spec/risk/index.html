    Spec — risk¶

    Slice 02-risk of RDM's spec, written the Löyly way in .pi/spec/risk/, read as the release gate reads it. Each user need is a screen of the event model, linked to its Allium surface; each accepted scenario, stamped with an id, is a requirement.

    User needs¶

    risk/risk-register¶

    A team can keep its risk register in the record, each risk scored from its own matrix and each control a verified requirement, and cannot release while a risk is unscored, uncontrolled, or residually unacceptable without a named acceptance.

    • Surface: RiskRegisterDesk
    • Facing: RiskOwner
    • Guarantees: MalformedRegisterBlocks, RegisterReadFromFrontmatter, RisksProjectedWithShapes, UncontrolledRiskBlocks

    spec.feature 
    Feature: Risk
  The accepted scenarios of this context: the risk register kept in the
  record, each risk scored only against the risk policy the project declares,
  each control a requirement of the spec, and the release held back while a
  risk is unscored, uncontrolled, or residually unacceptable without a named
  acceptance. Each is stamped with an item id that is never renumbered or
  reused, and verified by the acceptance tests whose Allure results carry
  that id as a tag label.

  Rule: The register is read from kind: risk frontmatter and evaluated only against a declared policy

    # risk.allium, surface RiskRegisterDesk, guarantee RegisterReadFromFrontmatter
    @allium-guarantee.RegisterReadFromFrontmatter
    @item-02-001
    Scenario: Every field of a risk is read, and its levels come only from the declared risk policy
      Given dhf/documents/risk/risks.md, of kind: risk and status proposed, declares RISK-A-1, RISK-A-2 and RISK-A-3
      And RISK-A-1 is a security risk with STRIDE category Spoofing, linked to RISK-A-2, hazard "Hz", situation "Si", harm "Ha", scored Serious × Possible with recorded level High, controlled by item-01-001 and item-01-002, with residual probability Unlikely, accepted by "QA lead" because "ALARP", and status approved
      And RISK-A-2 is a safety risk scored Serious × Possible, controlled by item-01-001, with residual Minor × Possible and no status of its own
      And RISK-A-3 is a safety risk scored Minor × Rare that nothing controls
      And dhf/documents/notes.md declares a risk RISK-NOT-1 but is not of kind: risk
      When the register is read with no risk policy declared
      Then it holds RISK-A-1, RISK-A-2 and RISK-A-3 and not RISK-NOT-1
      And every field of RISK-A-1 is read as written, its residual severity left unset, its document dhf/documents/risk/risks.md
      And RISK-A-2 has the document's status, proposed
      And no risk has a level or a residual level: nothing is evaluated without a policy
      When dhf/documents/risk/policy.md declares a risk policy with status proposed, in which Medium is acceptable only with a justification
      Then RISK-A-1 is High, residual Medium: its residual severity defaults to the initial Serious
      And RISK-A-2 is High, residual Medium, from its own residual Minor × Possible
      And RISK-A-3, uncontrolled, is Low and residual Low
      When the policy names its own levels Green, Amber and Red and a risk scored Bad × Often has residual probability Seldom
      Then that risk is Red, residual Amber
      When the policy gives no acceptability, leaves a severity and probability pair without a level, calls a level "fine", or lists a severity or probability twice
      Then the policy is refused, naming documents/risk/policy.md
      When a second document, documents/risk/second_policy.md, also declares a risk policy
      Then the policy is refused, naming both documents

    allium/risk.allium 
    -- allium: 3
-- .pi/spec/risk/allium/risk.allium
--
-- Scope: the risk context: the risk register kept in the record (the risks of every
-- kind: risk document's frontmatter), each risk scored only against the risk policy the
-- project declares, and the residual decision the release gate holds a release to. The
-- surface is the need this context is home to: risk-register (event-model/area.edn). RDM
-- ships no default policy.

------------------------------------------------------------
-- External Entities
------------------------------------------------------------

-- The spec RDM reads (specification): a risk's controls are its requirements.
external entity Spec {}

-- One executed test run (test_evidence): a control counts once it has a passing run.
external entity TestRun {}

-- The person who rates the risks and owns the register.
external entity RiskOwnerAccount {}

------------------------------------------------------------
-- Entities
------------------------------------------------------------

-- The project's risk policy, declared once (risk_policy in a document's frontmatter).
entity RiskPolicy {
    source: String
    severities: List<String>
    probabilities: List<String>
    levels: Map<String, List<String>>           -- one level for each severity and probability pair
    acceptability: Map<String, acceptable | justify | unacceptable>
    status: proposed | approved
}

-- One risk of the register, from the frontmatter of a kind: risk document.
entity Risk {
    id: String
    category: safety | security
    stride: String?                            -- a security risk's STRIDE category
    linked: List<Risk>
    hazard: String
    situation: String
    harm: String
    severity: String
    probability: String
    recorded_level: String?
    controls: List<String>                     -- requirement ids, item-NN-SSS
    residual_severity: String?                 -- defaults to the initial severity
    residual_probability: String?
    accepted_by: String?
    acceptance_rationale: String?
    status: proposed | approved                -- the document's when the risk gives none
    document: String
}

------------------------------------------------------------
-- Rules
------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------
-- Invariants
------------------------------------------------------------

invariant OneRiskPerId {
    for a in Risks:
        for b in Risks:
            a != b implies a.id != b.id
}

------------------------------------------------------------
-- Actor Declarations
------------------------------------------------------------

actor RiskOwner {
    identified_by: RiskOwnerAccount where true
}

------------------------------------------------------------
-- Surfaces
------------------------------------------------------------

-- A team can keep its risk register in the record, each risk scored from its own matrix
-- and each control a verified requirement, and cannot release while a risk is unscored,
-- uncontrolled, or residually unacceptable without a named acceptance.
surface RiskRegisterDesk {
    facing owner: RiskOwner
    context risk: Risk

    exposes:
        risk.id
        risk.category
        risk.severity
        risk.probability
        risk.controls
        risk.status

    @guarantee RegisterReadFromFrontmatter
        -- Every field of a risk is read from the frontmatter of a kind: risk document,
        -- and a risk is evaluated only against the one risk policy the project declares.

    @guarantee MalformedRegisterBlocks
        -- A register or policy that cannot be evaluated as written (a missing or
        -- duplicate id, an empty part, an unknown category, an undefined or mis-scored
        -- level, a control that is no requirement, controls with no residual) blocks
        -- release, naming the risk or the document.

    @guarantee UncontrolledRiskBlocks
        -- A residual not evaluated (a control with no passing test), unacceptable, or
        -- acceptable only with a justification nobody recorded blocks release, as does
        -- an unknown status; a proposed rating only warns.

    @guarantee RisksProjectedWithShapes
        -- The register is projected into the graph, each risk with its residual
        -- decision, and the graph's shapes block exactly what the release gate blocks.
}
