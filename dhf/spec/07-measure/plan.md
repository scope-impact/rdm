# Slice 07: Measure

## Goal

Someone other than the builder sees, cycle over cycle, how completely the spec carries the product, and what each run achieved, did outside its brief, and cost; the instrument never records what it did not measure.

## Scope

**In:**

- The checklist, derived from the cut (its regulated rule until 10-regulated-profile holds it)
- The scorer: a cycle is one directory; verdicts in one shape, from one or several graders; only a verified build scored
- The scoreboard: better and worse, never netted; a moved denominator said aloud
- Observe: each run's time, cost and what it touched outside its room

**Out:**

- The oracle, a plugin the project keeps: 08-plugins
