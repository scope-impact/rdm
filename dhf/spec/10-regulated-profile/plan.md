# Slice 10: The regulated profile

## Goal

A team building regulated software shows an auditor, for every clause it declares, the controlled document that meets it, and an audit copy with its evidence; a project that does not hold :regulated runs none of it.

## References

- UN-013 (`dhf/spec/needs.edn`)
- `dhf/wiki/pages/regulated-profile.md`

## Scope

**In:**

- The gap check: clause lists, audit tags, controlled documents' identity
- The audit: an auditor's and an engineer's copy of every document of a releasable record, with the evidence

**Out:**

- The checklist's and the release's regulated rules: in their components' slices until each component's feature file can sit here too (spec-30's build)

## Decisions

In the comments above the rules they shaped, each citing its incident (`README.md`, "A guard cites its incident"); a cycle's builder decisions are in `dhf/cycles/NN/decisions.yaml`.
