# How Part 11 and CSV fit: [CSA] §V.B (printed pp. 21-22) says a document
# required as evidence of validation and kept in electronic form is generally
# a Part 11 electronic record, so the system that keeps the validation record
# is held to Part 11 (the built-in part11_document_control checklist). For
# that system's own validation, Part 11 §11.10(a) asks for validation and
# [P11G] §III.C.1 asks that it rest on "a justified and documented risk
# assessment": the risk-based approach of [CSA], which this checklist follows.
#
# Source: [CSA] §V.A(1) Identifying the Intended Use, printed pp. 6-8 (PDF pp. 9-11)
CSA:intended-use the intended use of the software, and whether it is used directly as part of production or the quality management system, or to support them
# Source: [CSA] §V.A(2) Determining the Risk-Based Approach, printed pp. 8-11 (PDF pp. 11-14); high process risk defined on printed p. 9 (PDF p. 12)
CSA:risk the process risk: whether a failure of the software to perform as intended may result in a quality problem that foreseeably compromises safety (high process risk) or not
# Source: [CSA] §V.A(3) Production or Quality Management System Software Changes, printed pp. 11-12 (PDF pp. 14-15)
CSA:change assurance of a change to the software, and how the change is reported, in proportion to whether it may foreseeably compromise safety
# Source: [CSA] §V.A(4) Determining the Appropriate Assurance Activities, printed pp. 12-14 (PDF pp. 15-17); scripted and unscripted testing, printed p. 13 (PDF p. 16)
CSA:assurance assurance activities commensurate with the medical device risk (high process risk) or the process risk: scripted or unscripted testing, automated testing, ongoing monitoring
# Source: [CSA] §V.A(5) Additional Considerations for Assurance Activities, printed pp. 14-17 (PDF pp. 17-20)
CSA:assurance-considerations what else the assurance relies on: the supplier's own assurance, the software's own controls, and the digital evidence it keeps
# Source: [CSA] §V.A(6) Establishing the Appropriate Record, printed pp. 17-21 (PDF pp. 20-24); the record's contents, printed p. 17 (PDF p. 20); Table 1, printed p. 18 (PDF p. 21)
CSA:record the record: intended use, result of the risk-based analysis, the testing conducted, issues found, a conclusion of acceptability for the intended use with any issue's resolution, who performed it and when, review and approval
# Source: [CSA] §V.B Considerations for Electronic Records Requirements, printed pp. 21-22 (PDF pp. 24-25); [P11] §11.10(a)
CSA:part11 an electronic record required as evidence of validation is a Part 11 electronic record: the system that keeps it is held to Part 11, and validated as this checklist does
# Source: [ISO] 4.1.6, as cited by [CSA] §V.A(1), printed p. 6 (PDF p. 9)
13485:4.1.6-procedure a documented procedure for validating computer software used in the quality management system
# Source: [ISO] 4.1.6
13485:4.1.6-before-use validation before initial use and, as appropriate, after changes to the software or its application
# Source: [ISO] 4.1.6
13485:4.1.6-proportionate the approach and activities proportionate to the risk associated with the use of the software
# Source: [ISO] 4.1.6
13485:4.1.6-records records of the validation kept
# Source: [ISO] 7.5.6, as cited by [CSA] §V.A(1), printed p. 6 (PDF p. 9)
13485:7.5.6-software validation of computer software used in production, before initial use and after changes
