## Question

`HARM.severity_level` and `RISK_ASSESSMENT.severity_level` are both severity. `PROBABILITY_OF_HARM.p1_level`/`p2_level` and `RISK_ASSESSMENT.probability_level` are both probability. Which is authoritative, and what is the other for?

The plausible reading is that the assessment freezes the values at scoring time while the harm carries the current one — but that is unstated, and two unreconciled sources of truth for severity is precisely the drift the model cites as the reason not to inline harm text ("lets severity values diverge").

Decide, and state the invariant explicitly so it can become a validation rule rather than a convention.
