## Question

Specify how an ordered set of `MEASURE_EFFECT` rows folds into a residual `RISK_ASSESSMENT`.

The source model states relative effects chain and an absolute effect discards everything before it, with `sequence` ordering them — and calls this the subtlest part of the model. Open: what happens when two absolute effects target the same pathway; whether an effect can raise risk as well as lower it (see the location-permission case); whether the fold is total (always produces a residual) or partial (can produce "not assessable"); and whether `RISK_ASSESSMENT.stage` beyond {initial, residual} is needed for a multi-step reduction.

Today the fold is human judgement in prose: 56 wallet control bullets collapse to 14 residual scores with nothing checking the step. By contrast all 14 *initial* scores recompute exactly from the RMP-001 matrix, so the initial half is already machine-checkable and the residual half is not.
