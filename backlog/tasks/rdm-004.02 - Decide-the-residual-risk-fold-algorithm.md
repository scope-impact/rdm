## Question

Specify how an ordered set of `MEASURE_EFFECT` rows folds into a residual `RISK_ASSESSMENT`.

The source model states relative effects chain and an absolute effect discards everything before it, with `sequence` ordering them — and calls this the subtlest part of the model. Open: what happens when two absolute effects target the same pathway; whether the fold is total (always produces a residual) or partial (can produce "not assessable"); and whether `RISK_ASSESSMENT.stage` needs values beyond {initial, residual} for a multi-step reduction.

Today the fold is human judgement in prose: 56 wallet control bullets collapse to 14 residual scores with nothing checking the step. By contrast all 14 *initial* scores recompute exactly from the `RMP-001` matrix, so the initial half is already machine-checkable and the residual half is not.

## Constraints from the governing process

Established while checking `RMP-001` (revision 2) behind RDM-004.01. Both bear directly on this ticket.

**A computed residual is a proposal, not the residual.** `RMP-001` "Evaluate Residual Risk" applies **ALARP** and requires recording why further reduction is not reasonably practicable, what monitoring will detect issues, and **who approved the acceptance**. The process residual therefore contains an acceptance decision that no fold can produce. So the fold must emit a *proposed* residual, and the schema needs somewhere to hold the accepted value, its ALARP justification and its approver — which is probably a further `RISK_ASSESSMENT` stage rather than a column. Decide that here.

**The process control taxonomy has three modes, the model has two.** `RMP-001` "Control Risks" prioritises **Eliminate** (remove the hazard by design), **Reduce** (lower severity or probability), then **Inform** (warn the user of remaining risk). `MEASURE_EFFECT.mode` as drawn carries only relative and absolute. Eliminate maps to absolute and Reduce to relative, but **Inform maps to neither** — a warning changes no probability. So the fold needs an explicit no-change mode, or Inform-type measures will fold as an unquantified reduction they do not deliver. This is the likely source of silent residual inflation.

## Settled upstream (do not reopen)

From [decision-001](../decisions/decision-001%20-%20Mitigation-effects-attach-to-the-harm-pathway-not-the-hazardous-situation.md): effects attach to `PROBABILITY_OF_HARM`; one `MEASURE` carries several effect rows; effects are derived from control position with annotation as override; effects are reducing-or-neutral and never signed; an effect targets `p1_level` or `p2_level` and never severity; recorded residuals are provisional.
