## Question

Does `MEASURE_EFFECT` reference `PROBABILITY_OF_HARM` (one specific harm pathway) or `HAZARDOUS_SITUATION` (the situation as a whole)? The source model flags this as its own open question and notes retrofitting either direction is painful.

Evidence already in hand, from the halla-health wallet register: "Declining the location permission disables only these cards" appears as a control on two risks arising from the *same* hazardous situation (device-direct home-screen lookups to WAQI and NDMA Sachet). On RISK-NET-002 (harm: coarse location and IP to a third party) declining is a mitigation, residual Low. On RISK-SAFETY-005 (harm: delayed protective action in an emergency) declining is what removes the alert card, residual Medium. One mechanism, opposite valence, two harms, one situation.

Decide whether that case is decisive for harm-pathway granularity, or whether it is rare enough that situation-level granularity plus an exception mechanism is the better trade against row count.
