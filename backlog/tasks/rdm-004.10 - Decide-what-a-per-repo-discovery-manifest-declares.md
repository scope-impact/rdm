## Question

rdm hardcodes discovery in at least four places, and each one silently reports "nothing found" rather than failing: risk clusters are globbed from `backlog/docs/**/*RC-*.md` (the estate keeps them in `dhf/risk/` and `dhf/documents/risk/`); `scan_design_inputs` uses `repo_path / "dhf"` (the wallet DHF is at `halla_health_wallet/dhf`); `TEST_FILE_GLOBS` covers .py/.js/.ts/.java/.go but not YAML (infra 31 real `tags: [DI-n]` assertions therefore scan as zero); and `ID_PREFIXES` is eight literal prefixes, none of which any product in the estate uses for its documents.

Decide what a manifest declares, where it lives, and what happens when it is absent — in particular whether a repo with no manifest is an error or falls back to today defaults. Related: whether recognising zero items should be a hard failure, since `audit.py` currently scores an empty requirement set as 100 percent coverage and returns Grade A.
